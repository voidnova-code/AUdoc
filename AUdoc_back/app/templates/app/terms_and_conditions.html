2Description of Services

AUdoc is a campus healthcare portal built to make health services on campus easier to access and manage. Through the Portal, you can:

  • Book medical appointments with the campus health centre on a first-come, first-served basis
  • Register as a blood donor and respond to blood requests through a tiered urgency system
  • Request blood in emergency or planned situations, with escalation based on urgency
  • Interact with an AI-assisted chatbot for general guidance and portal navigation
  • Manage your health profile and appointment history

AUdoc is a coordination and scheduling tool. It does not itself provide medical treatment, diagnosis, or emergency response — these are carried out by the campus health centre and affiliated staff.

6Blood Bank & Donations

AUdoc's blood bank feature connects registered donors with requesters through a four-tier urgency and escalation system. By registering as a donor, you consent to being contacted when a compatible request matches your profile. Donation itself remains voluntary at every step — registering does not obligate you to donate, and you may decline or withdraw at any time.

Important: For genuine medical emergencies, contact the campus health centre or local emergency services directly rather than relying solely on the Portal's blood request feature, as response times depend on donor availability.
